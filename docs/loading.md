# Loading

Order (`Game.boot` → `World.build`): engine → loading view (sky; the model code waits for its first frame) → ground (worker) ∥ homestead (staged, added at once) → forest → meadow, lake, life (they need `world.ground`, not its textures) → `releaseGeometry` → `Game.warmUp` (`collect()` rounds until nothing new, `prewarmUI` alongside, then frames until two come at a normal pace) → **ready** ("click to walk"; `game.complete`). Nothing compiles in play. After ready: the audio clips download, `sharpenTextures()` swaps every texture to the tier's size (`game.sharp`), then the load workers close.

Key art (`index.html` `#keyart`): the game's own render (inline placeholder, then preloaded AVIF/WebP, before any JS; slow CSS drift) covers the world until ready, then cross-fades (1.4 s) to the start view. Phone portrait (≤ 4:5) has its own framing. Under it the loading view renders at half resolution, one frame when a stage joins the scene (else every 0.5 s), and whole shaders build in 40 ms slices (`sync`); `warmUp` restores full resolution. `?auto` hides it (full-resolution loading).

## Rules
- Only the homestead stage adds lights (≤2 point lights per model: `docs/materials.md`).
- `stream.js` builds each shader with the scene lights captured when it was queued: outside a render three leaves a scene's `LightsNode` stale (houses then lose their fire light).
- three keys a build by node identity: `shareGraphs` (`master.js`) makes same-shape materials share one graph before they join the scene (`docs/materials.md`).
- Await `world.breathe()` between load steps and `world.prewarm(scene, cam, target)` before an offscreen bake render.
- Anything added to the scene before `warmUp` is built before play; a material added later compiles in play.
- No WebGPU validation errors: three marks a pipeline failed (never drawn) on any error in its scope.
- Pure-CPU load work over ~50 ms: a worker job or a bake tool; dense per-vertex terrain lookups use `terrain.heightFast`.
- Pages caches files 10 min and replaced public files keep their names: packs and atlases load with `?k=<key>`; textures, sounds, card images and UI art with `?v=` their folder's content hash (`window.__ASSET_V__`, from `vite.config.js`; new loads go through `versioned()` or `sized()`); manifests with `cache: 'no-cache'`.
