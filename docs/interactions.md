# Interactions

Code: `src/interact/rites.js` (choron, lake cup, khomus); input flow in `src/main.js` (`act`, `put`, `run`, `inspect`, `read`, `actOf`, `closeUI`, prompt in `frame`), `src/input.js` (`doing`, `setDo`, `put`); windows in `src/ui/ui.js`, drawn cursor in `src/ui/cursor.js`, journal finder in `src/ui/guide.js`; hands-on logic in `src/interact/` (`index.js` wiring + berries, `hands.js` carrying, `seat.js` sitting, `work/` jobs); props in `src/models/chores.js` and `src/models/work.js` (built before the warm-up, so no runtime shader compiles).

## Inputs
| | Keyboard and mouse | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| **Do** (the hands): the act of the thing in view; with nothing in view, what the carried thing does by itself. Held where the motion lasts (`input.doing`) | left button (F) | the hand button, held; a drag that starts on it looks, so a held tool can be aimed. A short tap on a thing does its act, or shows its card when it has none |
| **Put down**: the carried thing back to its place when that is within reach (`thing.put()`: axe → block, scythe → post, bark → hide), else onto dry ground ahead; of an armful, one billet | right button (Q) | the put-down button (shown while carrying) |
| **Inspect**: the thing comes up (the inspect stage, `docs/ui.md`), never an act | E | the eye button (shown when the thing in view has a card) |

- The prompt names the thing in view, then the mouse glyph with what the hands would do, then an E chip when it has a card (no glyphs on touch: its buttons light up). The dot becomes a ring when the hands can do something; with nothing in view that ring is the only sign (no pill).
- An act never opens a card; doing a thing still adds its entry to the journal.
- One thing in the hands at a time (or an armful of billets); nothing goes into the lake.

## Flow
- E on a thing: the **inspect stage** (`docs/ui.md`): the view goes to the thing, with its name and one line (`mode: 'peek'`); E again → its text beside it (`'full'`); E again → closed, the view back in the body. You stand still throughout; W A S D, Esc or the close button also close. `game.inspect(it)` is the one entry point; `game.read(id, it, back)` opens an entry (no `it`: its own view, as from the journal).
- Stage and journal are mouse-driven: a drag on the open view turns the thing, the wheel moves nearer or scrolls the sheet under the cursor, photographs, names, related links, the close and language buttons. Keys: E next step, ← → turn, PgUp/PgDn/↑/↓ scroll, `[` `]` photographs, L language, J the journal. Phones: drag to turn, native scrolling, the touch close + language buttons.
- **Pointer lock is taken once** (title click) and kept: browsers show their "Esc to return the mouse" banner on every grant. While a window or the pause menu is up, `movementX/Y` drive a drawn cursor (`#vcursor`) that hit-tests with `elementFromPoint` and dispatches clicks (sliders drag, a `<select>` steps to its next option); `.vh` / `.vdown` stand in for :hover / :active.
- **Pause:** P keeps the lock (`game.pause()`, `ui.setMenu(true)`): Continue / P / a backdrop click just hides the menu, no banner. Esc or losing focus: the browser drops the lock (no browser lets a page keep it), any window closes, the menu shows, and Continue re-locks exactly once (`Input.locking`: one request in flight; the menu hides only when the lock is granted). Safari therefore shows its banner only after an Esc. Keyboard Lock (`navigator.keyboard.lock(['Escape'])`) is not used: Chromium only, and only in element fullscreen, which the game has none of. **Exit** (`game.exit()`): saves, `window.close()` (refused only in a tab holding an earlier page), otherwise the title screen and back to that page (not when it is the game again).
- Acts run while you walk and look. Only `act.freeze: true` holds the player: the ribbon choice (serge, sacred birch), the birch bow, the 0.9 s turn as you sit.
- `ui.showEntry(id, { mode: 'peek'|'full', kind })`, `ui.close()`, `ui.scroll(dy)`, `ui.modal` (journal or a card up: player and focus paused), `ui.onAction('close'|'lang'|'guide'|'learn'|'more'|'open', id, it, back)` (buttons, journal tiles, card links, names on the stage → main.js), `game.stage` (`on`, `open`, `close`, `orbit`, `zoomBy`), `game.paused`.
- Interactable hooks: `off` (not focusable), `take`, `berries`, `sit`, `fireplace: { pos(), upright, fuel() }`. `game.interact.actFor(it)` overrides `it.act` while carrying; `idleAct()` is the do input with nothing in focus; `putAct()` the put-down. `game.doReady` / `readReady` / `putReady` light the touch buttons. `game.choosing` = a `choose()` prompt is up (touch hides its controls). Things with a home (`hands.homed`): `guest` (choron), `offering` (lake stone), `khomus` flags on their interactables, parts in the model's `userData`.

## Journal finder
- Journal (`docs/ui.md`): pictures of the found things, the rest dim with "?"; found ones open their entry's own view on the stage. Choosing a "?" shows its where-to-look hint (`src/data/hints.json`, sah/en/ru by hand) and (`game.setGuide(id)`, again to drop) puts a gold diamond with the distance over its nearest place, clamped to the screen edge with an arrow when out of view, plus a soft shimmer within 7 m. Cleared when found. DOM only.
- Places come from `world.interactables` (bounding-box centre; zones at their centre). Entries with no place (`mogol-urasa`, `ornament`, `urung-aar-toyon-aiyy`, `baay-bayanay`, `algys`, `ysyakh`, `osuokhai`, `olonkho`) are found by following their dashed related link in a card that names them (`.chip.told`); the finder points at the nearest such card's place.
- Winter place (кыстык, `sayylyk-kystyk`): no marker unless chosen. In-world hint `src/interact/glint.js`: a brief additive star on the winter balagan's west window every 5–12 s, seen from 40–400 m while the sun lights that wall and it faces you; never once found. One sprite added before the warm-up; only its opacity uniform and scale change.
- First visit: language from `navigator.languages` (`detectLang` in `i18n.js`: `sah` anywhere → Sakha; first language ru / be / uk → Russian; else English). A pick on the title or in settings sets `save.langSet` and wins from then on.

## Actions
Tap = the do input pressed and let go; hold = kept down. Nothing here has a fail state.

| Action | Where | The hands | The world answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doors | ampaar, balagan | tap: a push; a push on the way turns the leaf back | it gathers speed by its weight, knocks on its stop and settles (`swing` in `yard/bits.js`); passable from ~0.6 open |
| Gate rails (сүрдьүгэс) | күрүө | hold: the rails are drawn out of the south post (0.8 s); let go early and they slide back in. Closing: a tap | once free they swing aside by their weight and knock; closing swings them back and runs them home |
| Мас ыаҕас | by the uraha door | tap takes it; put down anywhere dry. At water, looking down: hold dips it, and it fills while held there (a quick dip ≈ half, 1.2 s full). Looking down with water in it: hold tips it out, let go keeps the rest | the water stands as high as it was filled; the pail hangs lower and trails the view more the fuller it is; a strand runs from the rim while it pours |
| Firewood | woodpile, loose billets, nine dry branches under the north trees (`gather.js`, `save.work.drywood`) | on the pile: tap takes a billet, hold an armful (≤4); with billets in the arms tap stacks one, hold the next ones; put down by the pile stacks them all, elsewhere drops one. On a hearth: tap lays one | each billet knocks; the view dips as the load comes on; a laid billet burns down over 60 s and the fire is brighter (`fireplace.fuel()`); a branch lies in the forest again 5 min after it was used |
| Sit / stand | every орон bench | tap on the bench; tap with nothing in view, a step or a jump stands up | the view turns to the room and lowers |
| Дьэдьэн | 8 patches round the yard | tap picks one berry, hold strips the patch berry by berry | the berries go from the plant (into the тордуйа when one is carried); full again 4 min after the last one |
| Tar tub lid | ampaar | tap, once | the lid stays aside |
| Feed the fire | uraha hearth, summer balagan hearth (empty hands) | tap | a pinch flies from the hand in an arc to the fire; it flares and hisses on arrival |
| The guest's choron (`rites.js` `Choron`) | the board in the kymys corner | tap on it: the ladle fills it and it comes into the hands. Then anywhere: tap = a flick that sprinkles a few drops where you look (fire, earth, sky: the player's own aim), hold = it comes to the lips and tips while held. Put down (or tap on the board): back in its place | held upright whatever the view does, its kymys level; drops fly; aimed at the hearth, the fire flares as they land; drinking stops at what it held before the ladle; it goes back by itself when you step out of the uraha |
| Lake milk (`Cup`) | the cup on the offering stone | tap takes it; looking down at the water, hold tips it for as long as held; put down: back on the stone | a white strand from the rim, rings on the water; the milk in the cup shrinks and what is left stays; only over water (nothing to do on dry ground); empty for the rest of the visit |
| Khomus (`Khomus`) | its peg in the summer balagan | tap takes it to the lips; then tap = one pluck, hold = the playing goes on while held (and takes up where it stopped); put down: back on the peg | the frame hums after a stroke; not after dark (no act: the card says why) |

- What is carried has weight (`hands.js`): it trails the turning view, sits nearer the middle on a narrow screen, and `weigh()` dips the view when a load comes on.

## Work (`src/interact/work/`)
- A placed model names its job in `object.userData.work.job` (`JOBS` in `index.js`).
- `Work` (`index.js`, `game.work`): jobs are asked for an act before the carrying code (`actFor(it)` → act, `null` = nothing, `undefined` = not mine; `idleAct()`); `count(key)` → `save.work`; `say()` = a saying, once.
- Tools are carried things: `hands.addKind(kind, hold, lie)`, `hands.addThing({ kind, obj, it, name })`, `it.thing`, `thing.put()`; `hands.pose(dt, cam)` → `{ p, q, world? }` drives the held tool during a job.
- A job's movable parts are scene children marked `userData.dynamic`; after moving one, clear its `userData.sphere` (the look-at cache).
- Raise-and-release tools (axe, scythe): the do input held raises (≈0.7 s to the top, the hands come up to the face so the haft's height reads in view; kept up past ~2.5 s the arms tire and it sinks a little), letting go strikes where you look, put down while raised lowers it. A tap is a feeble blow.

## Left out on purpose (research "do not gamify")
- The forge work was left out at first (the smith's craft is close to the shaman's); added 2026-11-10 at the maintainer's request as the helper's part. The smith's initiation and the patron Кудай Бахсы stay on the card.
- Skimming stones on the lake (Эбэ, the lake's иччи, is "grandmother": no throwing things in), pushing the salama or ribbons (offerings, not toys), anything thrown into a fire except the offering itself (the pinch that feeds it, the drops of kymys) and a laid billet. No act has a fail state that could mock a rite: the choron, the lake's milk, the bow and the ribbon cannot go wrong.
