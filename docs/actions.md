# Actions

Tap = the do input pressed and let go; hold = kept down (`docs/interactions.md`). Nothing has a fail state.

| Action | Where | The hands | The world answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doors | ampaar, balagan | tap: a push; a push on the way turns the leaf back | it gathers speed by its weight, knocks on its stop and settles (`swing` in `yard/bits.js`); the leaf is solid wherever it stands and stops at the player (`leaf`) |
| Gate rails (сүрдьүгэс) | күрүө | hold: drawn out of the south post (0.8 s), let go early and they slide back; closing: a tap | once free they swing aside and knock; closing runs them home |
| Мас ыаҕас | by the uraha door | tap takes it; put down anywhere dry. At water, looking down: hold dips it and it fills while held (quick dip ≈ half, 1.2 s full). With water, looking down: hold tips it out, let go keeps the rest | the water stands as high as filled; the fuller pail hangs lower and trails more; a strand runs from the rim while it pours |
| Firewood | woodpile, loose billets, nine dry branches under the north trees (`gather.js`, `save.work.drywood`) | on the pile: tap takes a billet, hold an armful (≤4); with billets, tap stacks one, hold the next; put down by the pile stacks all, elsewhere drops one. On a hearth: tap lays one | each billet knocks; the view dips as the load comes on; a laid billet burns down over 60 s, the fire brighter (`fireplace.fuel()`); a branch lies again 5 min after use |
| Sit / stand | every орон bench | tap on the bench; tap with nothing in view, a step or a jump stands up | the view turns to the room and lowers |
| Дьэдьэн | the crowns of 24 colonies: yard margins, the slope above, the forest's edge | tap picks one berry, hold strips the crown | berries leave the plant (into a carried тордуйа); full again 4 min after the last |
| Tar tub lid | ampaar | tap, once | the lid stays aside |
| Feed the fire | uraha hearth, summer balagan hearth (empty hands) | tap | a pinch flies to the fire; it flares and hisses |
| The guest's choron (`rites.js` `Choron`) | the board in the kymys corner | tap: the ladle fills it, it comes into the hands. Then tap = a flick sprinkling drops where you look (fire, earth, sky: the player's aim); hold = to the lips, tipping while held. Put down (or tap the board): back in place | held upright, its kymys level; aimed at the hearth the fire flares; drinking stops at what it held before the ladle; it goes back by itself when you leave the uraha |
| Lake milk (`Cup`) | the cup on the offering stone | tap takes it; looking down at water, hold tips it; put down: back on the stone | a white strand, rings on the water; what is left stays; only over water; empty for the rest of the visit |
| Khomus (`Khomus`) | its peg in the summer balagan | tap takes it to the lips; tap = one pluck, hold = playing goes on (resumes where it stopped); put down: back on the peg | the frame hums after a stroke; not after dark (the card says why) |
| Ribbon | salama on the serge; the sacred birch | serge: tap. Birch: hold = the bow (head down while held, a tap is a nod). Then three strips under the look: mouse, ← → or 1 2 3 choose, do ties, put down / Esc leaves (touch: tap a strip or ✕) | the ribbon unrolls from the knot; kept in the save |

## Jobs (`src/interact/work/`)
Raise-and-release tools (axe, scythe): hold raises (≈0.7 s to the top; held past ~2.5 s it sinks), letting go strikes where you look, put down while raised lowers it; a tap is a feeble blow.

| Job | Where | The hands | The world answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Мас хайытыы (`split.js`) | chopping block by the woodpile | tap: the axe from the block, a round from the heap onto the block, a fallen half back onto it; hold and let go on the round: the blow; put down by the free block: the axe back into it | in line with the dark crack (≤6 cm off, ≤34° turned) and from high enough (round ½, half ⅓ of the swing) it opens: round → 2 halves → 2 billets each; a clean blow drops them by the block, a ragged one sends them tumbling. Low but in line: the axe bites in, hold lifts the round, letting go slams it open. Across the grain: stuck, a tap wrenches it free. Beside the round: bites the block. Two rounds (8 quarters): stack or burn the billets to free them (`save.work.rounds`, `billets`, `stacked`) |
| Туу (`fish.js`) | weir in the lake shallows, east of the net rack (`fishery.js`) | tap: take the trap, set it in the gap. Hold on the set trap: it comes up (full: slower), let go early and it settles back. Hold on the тымтай: the trap tips, the catch slides out fish by fish | мунду come in over minutes (~110 s to two thirds) and show through the wicker; the carrier fills (`save.work.mundu`) |
| От охсуу (`hay.js`) | the scythe post by the hay meadow fence (`hayfield.js`) | tap takes the саха хотуура; anywhere, hold and let go: the blow; put down by the post hangs it up | mows the real meadow in a half-circle (`world._field.mask.mow`): from the top 2.1 m and ±60°, low down to 0.9 m and ±27°; cut wisps lie where the blade went (`save.work.mown` m², `blows`) |
| Туос үлэтэ: тордуйа (`tuos.js`) | the bark sheet on the craft spot's hide (`barkwork.js`) | tap takes the sheet; hold at a hearth fire: turned to the heat while held; put down by the hide (or tap the craft spot): laid on it; hold on the laid sheet: a wall comes up and stays once ¾ up, else springs back; long sides first, then each end with its corner flaps and pegs | the long edges lift as it warms (soft from 1.3 s, for 150 s); past 2.9 s it curls tight, dries hard and must be warmed again; stiff bark lies flat and a fold gives a finger's width. The тордуйа is carried; дьэдьэн picked while it is held drop in (`save.work.torduya`, `berries`) |
| Уус кыһата (`yardlife/smithy.js`) | the smith's lean-to | the helper's part. Hold on the forge: a bag pressed while held (the bags take turns). Tap the glowing blank: tongs to the anvil. On the anvil hold and let go: the hammer. Tap dark iron: back to the coals. After the third pass, tap: quenched in the tub | coals and fire light answer the blast, the blank shows its heat in its colour (out from red, `NEED` 0.58). A full blow on bright iron counts whole and throws sparks, a light one half, dark iron only rings dull; five whole blows a pass, the prompt names the pass (уһугун тарт, хаптат, биитин таптай), the blank changes shape; about four blows a heat (`it.live()`; `save.work.hammer`, `blades`) |
| Кымыһы хамнатыы (`urasa.js` `churn`) | the симиир in the uraha | hold on the sack lifts the хамнатар, letting go plunges it | a stroke from the top counts whole, a short one in part; the sack swells; froth rises at the collar with the strokes and falls if left (12 whole strokes: it foams over and stays 90 s; `save.work.churn`, `kymys`) |

- Journal: a picture per job (`work/list.js` `WORKS`); a tap shows its counts, or where it waits.

## Left out on purpose (research: "do not gamify")
- The forge is played only as the helper's part (the smith's craft is close to the shaman's); the smith's initiation and the patron Кудай Бахсы stay on the card.
- Skimming stones (Эбэ, the lake's иччи, is "grandmother": nothing is thrown in), pushing the salama or ribbons (offerings, not toys), anything put into a fire but the offering itself (the pinch, the drops of kymys) and a laid billet. No rite can go wrong: the choron, the lake's milk, the bow and the ribbon have no fail state.
