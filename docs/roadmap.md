# Roadmap

Planned, not built. No people, no hunting, sacred acts explained never played (`AGENTS.md`). ★ = suggested first wave.

## Forest — animals
- ★ Squirrel (тииҥ): runs up a larch, chatters, drops a cone; forest edge.
- Hare (куобах): meadow edge at dusk, bolts when approached.
- Fox (саһыл): trots the treeline far off, stops, looks, leaves.
- Elk (тайах): rare silhouette at first/last light, gone if approached.
- ★ Bear signs only (clawed bark, dug anthill), never the bear; card on respect for Эһэ.
- ★ Woodpecker (тоҥсоҕой): drumming you can trace to a trunk, chips falling.
- Capercaillie / hazel grouse: flushes from the undergrowth.
- ★ Cuckoo (кэҕэ): calling deep in the forest; card (summer, fate).
- Owl: on a branch, head follows you.

## Forest — atmosphere
- Canopy wind waves, trunks creaking, sound leading the gust.
- Light shafts, drifting seeds and pollen in gaps.
- Mosquito and gnat clouds by damp hollows.
- Ground cover: ferns, moss, lingonberry, needles, game trails.
- Distant thunder and a passing summer shower (large).

## Lake — animals

- ★ Ducks with ducklings in a line, dabbing and diving.
- ★ Swan pair (куба) far out, never disturbed; card (sacred bird).
- Grebe diving and resurfacing elsewhere.
- ★ Heron / crane wading the shallows, slow strike.
- Muskrat V-wake crossing.
- Frogs at evening, plop on approach.
- Dragonflies and water striders by the reeds.
