# Patterns

Every drawn ornament, its source and how faithful it is. Rule: an ornament is traced from an image of real Sakha work (photo overlay or measured geometry), never drawn from a description; what has no traceable source stays plain. Sakha motif names stay descriptive until a Sakha speaker confirms them.

- Cover bands: colours are the photo's ratios to its bark (ornament ≈ 0.25–0.29, strips 0.34 / 0.52) applied to the cover cream; arcade period 0.20 m (scaled by the door), zigzag 0.24 m; band height from the traced aspect.
- Cover pipeline: rectify a sheet (quad along the cone generatrix), normalise the period, align every repeat to a reference (x, y, tilt, skew, x scale by NCC) on a luminance darkness map (cream 0, cut bark 1), median, remove the motif's lean (mirror axis per row band), mirror each motif, threshold at half the local peak (0.3–0.5, keeps thin cross arms), `potrace -O 1` (~100 nodes per band); the zigzag strip is a fitted polygon (edges at half its plateau), not traced.
- Turned vessels (`tools/make-carving.mjs`): silhouette → radius per row; surface unwrapped to (angle, height) with the camera elevation fitted so grooves run straight.
- Proof sheets: source | refit over the median | comparison; deviation = distance between the refit and the source's edge by the same rule; overlays re-project the texture onto the unwrapped photo.

## Plain (no traceable source)
- Urasa lower hoop: sources say it was carved like the posts (ru.wikipedia «Ураса»; the Uran reconstruction shows an arcade frieze); no free image to trace.
- Balagan and urasa bench beams: Jochelson's «kardis oyu» (Fig. 93w) is a notched line on kumys vessels, not on beams.
- Serge small posts (`serge.js` `smallPost`): no source of their own; each repeats the main post's traced teeth band once under the neck (16 round), the top stays a plain knob.
- Coat breast and back bead panels: REM 8762-19876 (women's festive coat) shows bead-and-braid strips too small in its 1353 px photo to trace.
- The breast ornament (илин кэбиһэр): three chains of the traced кэлин кэбиһэр links to a bar and the күн disc, arranged after the entry's sources, not an image. Rys. 106 is a different piece (a silver-embroidered breastplate).
- Urasa cover, top garland band (Suntar photo: motifs ~6 px, unreadable).
- Balagan хаппахчы curtain (`balagan/mats.js` `birch-curtain`): plain boiled bark; Sieroszewski's interiors (Rys. 65, 68) show no ornamented curtain.
- Urasa door: the Suntar urasa has a plank door, no curtain to trace; no mica chips in the cover bands; ornamented sheets are cut straight (as the Suntar sheets).
- Not ornamented: `canvas_birch*`, `canvas_endgrain`, `canvas_cloth`, `canvas_silver`, `canvas_skin` (bark, grain, twill, sheet, skin); `index.html` UI.
