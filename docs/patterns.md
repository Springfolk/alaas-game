# Patterns

Every drawn ornament, its source and how faithful it is. Rule: an ornament is traced from an image of real Sakha work (photo overlay or measured geometry), never drawn from a description; what has no traceable source is left out. Sakha motif names stay descriptive until a Sakha speaker confirms them.

Band colours are the photo's ratios to its bark (ornament ≈ 0.25–0.29, strips 0.34 / 0.52), applied to the cover cream. Sizes: arcade period 0.20 m (scaled by the door), zigzag 0.24 m; band height from the traced aspect. Turned vessels (`tools/make-carving.mjs`): silhouette → radius per row; the surface is unwrapped to (angle, height) with the camera elevation fitted so grooves run straight; overlays in the proof sheets show the texture re-projected onto the unwrapped photo. Pipeline (cover): rectify a sheet (quad along the cone generatrix), normalise the period, align every repeat to a reference (x, y, tilt, skew, x scale by NCC) on a luminance darkness map (cream 0, cut bark 1), median, take out the motif's lean (mirror axis fitted per row band), mirror each motif about its axis, threshold at half the local peak (0.3–0.5, keeps thin cross arms), `potrace -O 1` (smooth Béziers, ~100 nodes per band instead of 126–204); the zigzag strip is a fitted polygon (edges at half its own plateau), not traced. Proof sheets: source | refit over the median | before vs after; deviation = distance between the refit and the source's edge by the same rule.

## Left out (no traceable source yet)

- Urasa posts (`urasa/mats.js` `post-stained`): plain since the zigzag and hatched-triangle bands had no image source (only a ru.wikipedia description); no photo or drawing of carved urasa posts found (Jochelson, Sieroszewski, REM, Commons).
- Balagan bench beams: plain (the notched band with rhombs had no source; Jochelson's «kardis oyu», Fig. 93w, is a notched line on kumys vessels, not on beams). Urasa bench beams were already plain.
- Serge small posts: plain (their rings had no source).
- Coat chest beadwork band with silver plates (balagan): removed; REM 8762-19876 (women's festive coat) shows bead-and-braid strips along the front edge, cuffs and hem instead, too small in its 1353 px photo to trace. The breast ornament (илин кэбиһэр: chains, bars, pendants, disc) is plain geometry, not ornament; Rys. 106 is a different piece (a silver-embroidered breastplate).
- Urasa cover, top garland band (Suntar photo: motifs ~6 px, unreadable).
- Balagan хаппахчы curtain (`balagan/mats.js` `birch-curtain`): plain boiled bark; its black openwork bands had no source, and Sieroszewski's interiors (Rys. 65, 68) show no ornamented curtain.
- Urasa door curtain embroidery (was the cover's triangle band; the Suntar urasa has a plank door, no curtain to trace); mica chips in the cover bands; scalloped edges of ornamented sheets (Suntar sheets are cut straight).

Not ornamented: `canvas_birch*`, `canvas_endgrain`, `canvas_cloth`, `canvas_silver` (plain bark, grain, twill, sheet); balagan silver other than the belt plates; `index.html` UI.
